Questions we hear


Which models and tools do you use?

Whatever fits: hosted APIs, open-weight models or a mix. We test several on your data before committing.

How long until we see something working?

Usually a prototype within two to three weeks of kickoff.

Do we need clean data first?

Not necessarily. The assessment shows what is needed, and we often fix data in parallel.

What happens to our data?

It stays in your environment wherever possible, and we agree handling rules before work starts.

Tell us what you are building.

A 30-minute discovery call. You describe the problem; we tell you honestly whether and how AI fits.
